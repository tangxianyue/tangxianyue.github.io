I am **Xianyue Tang**, a PhD student in Urban Studies at **The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen)**. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Urban and Rural Planning from Central South University and a Master's degree in Architecture from Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen).

## Research Interests

My research interests primarily include **urban spatial perception and behavior, urban regeneration in informal settlements, and urban green space planning**.

I am particularly interested in the interactions between the built environment, individual perceptions and behaviors, and socio-spatial relationships, aiming to provide scientific evidence for human-centered urban planning, urban regeneration, and public space optimization.

My research integrates interdisciplinary approaches, including **GIS, GeoAI, virtual reality (VR) and machine learning**.

## Future Research & Collaboration

I aim to further explore the dynamic relationships between urban spatial perception and pedestrian mobility.

I welcome **interdisciplinary academic exchanges and research collaborations** with scholars from diverse fields.

## Beyond Academia

Outside academia, I enjoy **Minecraft, cycling, hiking, and music**.
